What is TT Schedule?

2 / 15

Purpose

TT Schedule is a visual planning tool that lets you assign open trouble tickets to field engineer teams across a 14-day calendar. It is designed to replace any in-house scheduling you currently do.

  • ✓
    See all open tickets for your country in one place
  • ✓
    Drag tickets directly onto the calendar for a team
  • ✓
    Plan team movements with a live map view
  • ✓
    Schedule non-work events (leave, training, travel, etc.)
  • ✓
    Changes save automatically — nothing to export
Who uses it? Lead Field Engineers and Country Managers who are responsible for dispatching teams to sites.

Access

Navigate to AMN Portal → FE Tools → FE Scheduler from the main menu. You must be logged in with an internal account.

🗺️

Interface Overview

3 / 15

Four Key Areas

  • A
    Events Panel Coloured chips for non-ticket events (leave, training, travel, etc.). Drag these onto the calendar.
  • B
    Open Tickets Tray All unresolved AMN Portal tickets for your country, sorted by priority. Drag cards onto the calendar to schedule them.
  • C
    Map View Shows BTS sites with open tickets plotted on a map. Markers are colour-coded by priority. You can also drag markers onto the calendar.
  • D
    Timetable 14-day grid with one row per team. Drop tickets and events here to build the schedule.

Getting Started

4 / 15

Step by step

1
Open TT Schedule Navigate to AMN Portal → FE Tools → FE Scheduler from the main portal navigation.
2
Select your country Use the Country dropdown in the top-right of the header bar. Only countries with open tickets will appear.
3
Wait for data to load The page fetches open tickets, field engineers, and any previously saved schedule simultaneously. This takes 1–3 seconds.
4
Review what loaded Check the ticket count badge, confirm the map has plotted markers, and verify that your teams appear in the timetable.
5
Start scheduling Drag tickets from the tray onto calendar cells. The schedule saves automatically after each change.

Scheduling a Ticket

7 / 15

How to schedule

1
Find the ticket in the tray Tickets are sorted by priority (urgent first). Hover over a card to highlight its location on the map.
2
Drag the card Click and hold the ticket card. The cursor changes to a grab hand. Drag it towards the timetable.
3
Drop onto the correct team & date The target cell highlights with a blue dashed border when you hover over it. Release to drop.
4
Confirm it appeared The ticket entry appears in the cell. The tray card fades to show it is scheduled.
ETA set automatically: When you drag a ticket onto the schedule, the ETA date on the trouble ticket is set automatically to match the date you dropped it on. You no longer need to manually update the ETA on the ticket.
Same ticket, multiple teams: A ticket can only be scheduled to one team at a time. If it is already on the board, its tray card will be faded and cannot be dragged again. To move it to a different team, drag it directly from its current position in the timetable and drop it onto the desired team's cell.

Managing Teams

9 / 15

Team Actions

  • 1
    Rename a team Click the team name field and type directly. Changes save automatically.
  • 2
    Assign a Lead FE Use the Lead FE dropdown in the team cell. The list shows all field engineers registered for your country.
  • 3
    Add a team Click the + Add Team button at the bottom of the timetable. A new row is added immediately.
  • ×
    Remove a team Click the × button in the top-right of the team cell. At least one team must always remain. Removing a team also clears the ETA dates for any tickets it had scheduled.
Each team gets a unique colour (shown as the left border stripe). This same colour is used for route lines on the map.

Moving & Removing Entries

10 / 15

Moving an entry

1
Drag from within the timetable Any entry already on the calendar can be dragged to a different day or a different team. The cursor shows a grab hand.
2
Drop on the new cell Release over any cell. The entry disappears from its original location and appears in the new one.
3
ETA updates automatically For ticket entries, the ETA in AMN Portal is updated to match the new calendar day.
You cannot drop a ticket entry onto a cell that already contains the same ticket — the system ignores the duplicate drop.

Removing an entry

Click the × button on the right of any entry chip in the timetable. The entry is removed and the ticket's ETA in AMN Portal is cleared.

Team Route Planning

11 / 15

What the Route button does

Once a team has tickets scheduled across multiple days, the Route button draws a numbered travel line on the map showing the order of site visits, sorted by date.

1
Schedule tickets for a team across several days At least one site must have a known GPS location in the database.
2
Click the Route button Found in the team cell, bottom-right. It shows the team's colour and a route icon.
3
Review the route on the map A dashed line connects sites in chronological order. Each stop is numbered. Click a stop marker to see the site name and date.
4
Click Route again to hide The button toggles the route on and off. The map returns to normal markers.
If no sites have location data yet, a notification will appear: "No located sites assigned to this team yet."
Each team has a distinct colour. Up to 10 different team colours cycle automatically.
Pro tip: Use the route view to identify nearby sites that fall between scheduled stops — you may be able to add them to the trip and cover more tickets in a single visit, maximising team efficiency.

Navigating the Calendar

12 / 15

Default view

The timetable shows today + the next 13 days (14 days total). Today's column is highlighted in light blue.

Viewing the past 7 days

1
Click and drag the column header bar to the right The cursor changes to a left-right resize arrow when over the header. Drag right by more than 60px to switch to the past 7-day view.
2
The header hint changes A message in orange reads "Showing past 7 days". You can review what was scheduled last week.
3
Drag left to return to today Drag the header bar left by more than 60px to snap back to the current 14-day view.
The hint bar below the events/tray panel shows which view is active and how to navigate.

Auto-Save & Data Persistence

13 / 15

How saving works

Every change you make — adding a ticket, moving an entry, adding a team, removing an event — triggers an automatic save. You do not need to click a Save button.

1
Change is made Drag a ticket, update a team name, remove an entry, etc.
2
"Saving…" appears in the header A 1.5-second debounce timer starts. Rapid back-to-back changes are batched into a single save.
3
"Saved ✓" confirms success This message appears briefly and then disappears. If it shows "Save failed", check your network connection and try the change again.
Schedule persists across sessions. When you or another user opens TT Schedule for the same country, the previously saved schedule is restored exactly as it was.
ETA dates sync to AMN Portal. When a ticket is scheduled on a date, that date is written back to the ticket's ETA field in AMN Portal automatically.

Tips & Common Mistakes

14 / 15

Best Practices

  • ✓
    Schedule Urgent and High Value tickets first before filling other days.
  • ✓
    Use the map view to group nearby sites on the same day to minimise travel.
  • ✓
    Use the Route button to visually sense-check a team's travel plan before finalising.
  • ✓
    Add Travel or Prep-for-Trip events on days when the team is in transit.
  • ✓
    Mark Non-Working Days and Annual Leave so the timetable accurately reflects team availability.
  • ✓
    Check "Saved ✓" appears after major changes before closing the browser.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ✗
    Trying to drag a faded/scheduled ticket — it is already on the board. Find it in the timetable to move it instead.
  • ✗
    Closing the browser immediately after a drag — wait for "Saved ✓" to confirm the save.
  • ✗
    Removing the last team — the system will warn you; you must always have at least one team.
  • ✗
    Expecting the ticket count badge to decrease when you schedule a ticket — the count shows all open tickets, not how many are still unscheduled.
  • ✗
    Thinking the map shows all BTS sites — only sites with open tickets (and known GPS coordinates) appear as markers.
