Monster Sound Land!
A picture matching board game · Ages 4–7
How to Play
1Roll the dice and move your token that many spaces forward.
2If you land on a monster space, pick up a picture card from the pile.
3Look at the picture — does it match that monster's sound? If YES, keep the card and score a point!
4⭐ spaces: move forward 2!    💀 spaces: move back 2!
5First to FINISH wins — or whoever has the most cards!
